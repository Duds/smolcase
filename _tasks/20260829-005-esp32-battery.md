## Question

Which ESP32 module and battery configuration should SMOLCASE use for the BLE bridge and power system?

**Blocked by:** 20260829-003 — servo voltage and stall current drive the battery and regulator decisions.

**Research dimensions:**
1. **ESP32 module**: DevKit-C vs custom PCB vs Xiao ESP32-C3 vs equivalent. Must support BLE GATT server. Size constraints from SC-CASE internal volume.
2. **Battery**: LiPo pouch vs 18650 cell. Capacity target based on: Pixel 8 battery + ESP32 + 2x servos. Runtime target: ~2-4 hours of intermittent desk operation.
3. **Power regulation**: Buck converter to supply servo voltage from battery. Shared ground architecture. 5V rail for ESP32 if needed.
4. **Charging**: Onboard charging circuit (e.g. TP4056 for LiPo) or external charger.
5. **Connectors**: JST or similar for battery disconnect, servo connectors matching selected servo pinout.

**Resolution:** One ESP32 module selected, battery chemistry and capacity decided, buck converter/regulator IC chosen, charging strategy documented. All captured in `docs/04-hardware/` with schematic sketch.
